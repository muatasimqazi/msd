Humanitarian Work

Flood Relief and Ration Distribution Across Balochistan

Personally funded and led ration distribution for approximately 5,000 flood victims across Mekran and Bela, including 15-day ration packages for 1,000 flood-affected people.

The distribution effort was prominently supported by family members Mir Israr Ishaq Dashti, Yousaf Dashti, Zafer Eassa Dashti and Faraz Dashti, who helped deliver supplies directly to affected communities.
